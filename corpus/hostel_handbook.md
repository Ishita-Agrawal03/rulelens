# Northbridge Institute of Technology
## Hostel Handbook
**Version:** 2026.1

## 1. Purpose
This handbook establishes residence rules for students living in Institute hostels. It covers room allocation, entry, visitors, leave, common facilities, safety, discipline, maintenance, and emergency procedures.

## 2. Hostel Eligibility
Accommodation is subject to availability and the allocation procedure. Students must complete residence forms and provide accurate emergency contact information. Allocation may consider programme year and other criteria published by the Hostel Office.

## 3. Room Allocation
Rooms are assigned by the Hostel Office. Students may request a room change during the published request period. A change is effective only after approval and update of the hostel record.

## 4. Curfew
The standard hostel curfew is **10:00 PM**. Residents returning after curfew must use the designated late-entry process and may be required to provide a reason.

## 5. Late Entry
Students arriving after curfew must register their arrival. Repeated late entries may result in a warning or referral to the Hostel Discipline Committee.

## 6. Visitors
Visitors may enter only during published visiting hours and must register at the security desk. Overnight visitors are not permitted unless the Hostel Office grants written authorization under the applicable residence procedure.

## 7. Overnight Leave
Residents leaving campus overnight must submit the prescribed leave request. Approval may depend on residence arrangements and programme requirements.

## 8. Mess and Food
Residents must follow mess timings and hygiene requirements. Special dietary requests may be submitted to hostel administration but depend on feasibility and available facilities.

## 9. Room Maintenance
Students are responsible for reasonable care of assigned rooms and furniture. Damage beyond ordinary wear may be charged to the responsible resident after assessment.

## 10. Prohibited Conduct
Unauthorized dangerous items, harassment, deliberate property damage, unauthorized commercial activity, and conduct that materially disturbs residents are prohibited. Serious matters may be referred to the Institute disciplinary process.

## 11. Electrical Appliances
Only appliances identified as permitted by the Hostel Office may be used. High-load appliances may be prohibited for electrical safety.

## 12. Leave During Examinations
Students should check academic and hostel schedules before requesting leave during examination periods. Hostel approval does not change academic examination requirements.

## 13. Emergency Situations
During a verified emergency, security staff may temporarily modify entry procedures. An emergency measure should be recorded and does not permanently change the standard curfew.

## 14. Discipline
Warnings, fines, restrictions, or referral may be imposed depending on the seriousness and recurrence of conduct. Students should receive information about the applicable process.

## 15. Appeals
A student may appeal a hostel disciplinary decision when the applicable notice or process provides an appeal route.

## 16. Quiet Hours
Residents should maintain quiet during designated study and sleeping periods. Repeated disturbance may be treated as a residence conduct issue.

## 17. Guests During Events
Special residence arrangements may be announced for Institute events. Such notices should identify the dates and temporary visitor arrangements.

## 18. Security
Students should carry their residence identification when required. Lost identification should be reported to the Hostel Office or security desk.

## 19. Fire Safety
Fire exits and emergency equipment must remain unobstructed. Students must follow evacuation instructions during drills and emergencies.

## 20. Lost Property
Lost property found in common areas should be handed to the designated hostel office or security desk. Students claiming an item may be asked to provide identifying information.

## 21. Room Inspection
Authorized staff may inspect rooms under the hostel procedure for safety, maintenance, or investigation. Where advance notice is practicable, it should be provided according to the applicable procedure.

## 22. Closing
The Hostel Office may publish operational instructions consistent with this handbook. Students should follow the latest applicable operational notice while recognizing that a temporary instruction does not automatically amend a permanent handbook rule.

## 23. Laundry and Common Facilities
Residents must use laundry and common facilities according to posted schedules. Equipment should be left clean and available for other residents. Damage caused by misuse may be referred under the residence discipline procedure.

## 24. Study Rooms
Study rooms are shared facilities. Residents may be required to reserve them when demand is high. Personal belongings should not be left unattended for extended periods.

## 25. Internet and Network Use
Hostel internet is provided for academic and ordinary personal use subject to technology-use rules. Deliberate network disruption, unauthorized access, or attempts to bypass security controls may be referred to the relevant authority.

## 26. Maintenance Requests
A resident should report plumbing, electrical, furniture, or safety problems through the designated maintenance channel. Emergency faults should be reported immediately to hostel staff or security.

## 27. Room Keys
Students are responsible for assigned keys or access credentials. Lost keys should be reported promptly. Replacement charges may apply where the residence schedule provides for them.

## 28. Absence From Hostel
Students who will be away overnight should use the approved leave process. A parent or friend informing another resident does not constitute official leave approval.

## 29. Visitors and Identification
Visitors may be asked to provide identification and comply with security procedures. Residents are responsible for the conduct of visitors they sponsor.

## 30. Noise and Events
Residents organizing gatherings must comply with quiet-hour and event requirements. Approval for an event on a particular date does not permanently change ordinary residence rules.

## 31. Health and Safety
Students should report serious injuries, fire hazards, and urgent safety issues through designated channels. Staff may take temporary measures to protect residents.

## 32. Evacuation
During an evacuation, students must follow instructions from security and hostel staff and proceed to the designated assembly area. Personal belongings should not delay evacuation.

## 33. Food Storage
Food stored in rooms must be kept in a manner consistent with hygiene and hostel safety requirements. Perishable food should not be stored where it creates a sanitation problem.

## 34. Complaints
A resident may submit a complaint concerning facilities or conduct through the designated office. The complaint should identify the issue and relevant dates where possible.

## 35. Residence Notices
The Hostel Office may issue temporary operational notices for maintenance, examinations, festivals, or security events. Such notices should identify their period of operation.

## 36. Closing Principle
Students should distinguish permanent handbook rules from temporary operational instructions. When two provisions genuinely conflict and neither states which controls, the resident should seek clarification rather than assume that the later or more convenient rule applies.
