# Northbridge Institute of Technology
## Medical and Exceptional Circumstances Policy
**Policy ID:** NIT-ME-2026 | **Version:** 2026.1

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains procedures for students who experience documented medical circumstances or other exceptional situations that affect attendance, examinations, or academic deadlines. It does not create an automatic exemption for every personal difficulty.

## 2. Medical Grounds
Medical grounds may include hospitalization, serious illness, acute injury, or another condition supported by acceptable medical documentation. The Institute may request clarification when a certificate does not establish the period of incapacity or appears incomplete.

## 3. Medical Documentation
A medical certificate should identify the student, relevant dates, issuing medical practitioner or institution, and date of issue. Supporting records may be requested where necessary.

Students should submit medical documents through the official channel. A document submitted informally to a classmate or unrelated office should not be assumed to constitute a valid request.

## 4. Medical Attendance Exemption
A student seeking attendance consideration on medical grounds must submit the prescribed request to the department. The department reviews the attendance record and documentation and forwards the recommendation to the designated authority.

**Intentional evaluation provision:** Where a medical exemption is formally approved, this policy states that the student may be treated as eligible for the semester-end examination with attendance of **65% or above**.

The approval should be recorded before the student is treated as eligible under the medical exemption. The provision does not state that every medical certificate automatically produces an exemption.

## 5. Examination Absence
A student unable to attend an examination because of hospitalization or another documented medical emergency may request a deferred examination. The request should normally be submitted within three working days after the missed examination unless the student was medically incapable of submitting it.

The request should identify the missed examination and include the required documentation. Approval is determined under the applicable procedure.

## 6. Exceptional Circumstances
Exceptional circumstances may include circumstances specifically recognized by an institutional notice or committee decision. Serious medical emergencies and institute-representative obligations may qualify when the relevant process applies.

This policy does not establish an automatic academic exemption for family weddings, vacations, ordinary travel, celebrations, or convenience.

## 7. Department Review
The department verifies the student's course, attendance percentage, and documentation. It may request clarification. The department's recommendation is forwarded to the designated authority when approval is required.

## 8. Committee Review
The Exceptional Circumstances Committee may review cases referred by departments. Its decision should identify the provision considered and the relief granted. A decision for one student does not automatically amend a general policy.

## 9. Deadline Consideration
Medical consideration may be available for an academic deadline when the relevant policy permits an extension. A medical certificate alone does not automatically extend every Institute deadline.

## 10. Appeals
A student may appeal a rejection when the applicable process provides an appeal. The appeal should identify the original request and explain why reconsideration is requested.

## 11. Verification
The Institute may verify medical documentation where necessary. False or materially misleading documentation may be referred under the Student Code of Conduct.

## 12. Privacy
Medical records should be submitted through designated channels and handled according to institutional privacy procedures. Students should avoid sending sensitive records to public mailing lists or unrelated offices.

## 13. Decision Records
Approved exceptions should record the student, course or activity, relevant period, applicable provision, decision, and approving authority. Records should be sufficient for later administrative verification.

## 14. Non-medical Requests
A request based solely on convenience, personal travel, family functions, or social events should not be treated as a medical request. Students should identify another applicable policy if one exists.

## 15. Interaction With Other Regulations
This policy should be read together with Academic Regulations. Where provisions appear inconsistent and no explicit precedence rule is provided, the student should seek an institutional determination rather than assume that one document automatically overrides another.

## 16. Student Communication
Students should receive acknowledgement of a submitted request through the designated administrative process where such acknowledgement is provided. Students should retain the acknowledgement and decision.

## 17. Late Submissions
A late request may be considered when the student can establish a reason for delay and the applicable authority has discretion to accept it. Late submission does not guarantee approval.

## 18. Closing
The purpose of this policy is to provide a documented route for genuine medical and exceptional circumstances while preserving academic procedures. Students should rely on an approved decision rather than on informal assurances.

## 19. Medical Leave and Course Participation
Medical leave may affect attendance in lectures, laboratories, or other activities only through the approved process. Approval for examination absence does not automatically excuse attendance in every course. The decision should identify the relevant activity where possible.

## 20. Hospitalization
Where hospitalization prevents a student from making a timely request, the student or an authorized representative may contact the designated office when practicable. Evidence of the hospitalization period may be requested later. Hospitalization does not automatically determine the outcome of an academic assessment.

## 21. Injury and Temporary Restrictions
A temporary injury may support a request for reasonable academic consideration when the relevant process permits it. The student should provide documentation identifying the period during which participation was affected. The Institute may distinguish between inability to attend and inability to complete a particular activity.

## 22. Medical Review
For complex requests, the Institute may refer documentation to a designated medical authority. The authority may advise whether documentation supports the claimed period. The academic decision remains subject to the applicable policy and approving authority.

## 23. Documentation Authenticity
The Institute may verify documentation where necessary. A student should not alter a medical certificate. Suspected alteration may be handled under the conduct process.

## 24. Confidentiality
Only personnel who need information for processing a request should access medical documentation. Students should use secure institutional channels and should not circulate medical records unnecessarily.

## 25. Emergency Communication
If a student becomes medically incapacitated immediately before an examination, the student should notify the department or examination office as soon as reasonably possible. Emergency communication does not replace the formal application and documentation process.

## 26. Attendance Records After Approval
When a medical attendance request is approved, the department should record the decision against the relevant course or period. Students should verify the record after processing and report discrepancies.

## 27. Non-medical Exceptional Events
A committee may consider an exceptional event when another institutional policy grants it authority. This policy does not turn a family function, travel plan, or social obligation into a medical ground.

## 28. Pending Requests
A student whose medical request is pending should not assume that a shortage list has been automatically changed. The official attendance record should be corrected after an approved decision is communicated.

## 29. Clarification Requests
If a student cannot determine which medical process applies, the student should describe the situation to the designated office without assuming a particular outcome. The office may identify the appropriate procedure.

## 30. Closing Principle
Medical consideration is evidence-based and case-specific. An automated system should report policy language and approval status rather than infer that a medical certificate guarantees academic relief.
