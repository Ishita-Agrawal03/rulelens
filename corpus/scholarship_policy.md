# Northbridge Institute of Technology
## Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy
**Version:** 2026.1

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes general procedures for Institute-administered scholarships and financial aid. Individual scholarship announcements may contain additional conditions.

## 2. Eligibility
Eligibility may depend on academic performance, financial need, programme status, and award-specific conditions. Students should read the terms of the particular award.

## 3. Application
Applications must be submitted through the designated portal by the published deadline. Supporting documents should be complete and readable.

## 4. Academic Requirement
Where an award specifies an academic threshold, students must satisfy that threshold for renewal. The threshold may differ between awards.

## 5. Attendance
Some scholarships may include attendance requirements. Students should consult the terms of the individual award rather than assume that the general academic attendance requirement is identical to the scholarship requirement.

## 6. Financial Need
Need-based awards may require income or financial documentation. Updated documentation may be requested during renewal.

## 7. Renewal
Renewal is subject to verification of continuing eligibility. Receiving an award in one term does not guarantee renewal in the next term.

## 8. Suspension
An award may be suspended when eligibility conditions are not met, required documents are missing, or an award-specific rule provides for suspension.

## 9. Cancellation
Cancellation should follow the procedure specified in the award terms. Where cancellation is based on inaccurate information, the Institute may request an explanation.

## 10. Payment
Approved amounts are normally credited after verification and administrative processing. This policy does not guarantee a particular banking date unless the award notice states one.

## 11. Appeals
A student may appeal a scholarship decision when the award terms provide an appeal mechanism. The appeal should identify the award and decision.

## 12. Changes in Circumstances
Students should notify the Financial Aid Office when a material change affects eligibility. The office may reassess the award.

## 13. Multiple Awards
Students must disclose other institutional funding when the application requires it. Some awards may restrict simultaneous funding.

## 14. Academic Failure
The effect of a failed course depends on the terms of the particular scholarship. This policy does not establish a universal rule that every failed course causes cancellation.

## 15. Conduct
Scholarship recipients remain subject to student conduct rules. A conduct finding affects an award only where the scholarship terms or a competent institutional decision provides for that consequence.

## 16. Records
Financial aid records should be retained according to institutional recordkeeping procedures.

## 17. Disbursement Delays
Administrative verification, incomplete documents, banking errors, or other processing issues may delay payment. A delay does not by itself establish that an award has been cancelled.

## 18. Scholarship Committee
The Scholarship Committee may review applications and appeals within the authority assigned by the award programme. Committee membership does not imply authority to alter unrelated academic rules.

## 19. Fraud and Misrepresentation
Knowingly providing false financial or academic information may result in rejection, suspension, cancellation, or referral under another applicable procedure.

## 20. Interpretation
Questions about a particular award should be resolved using its published terms together with this general policy.

## 21. Application Review
The Financial Aid Office may review applications for completeness before substantive eligibility assessment. Missing information may result in a request for clarification. Students should respond within the period stated in the request.

## 22. Selection
Where applications exceed available funding, selection may use the criteria stated in the particular scholarship announcement. This general policy does not create a universal ranking formula.

## 23. Waitlists
A scholarship programme may maintain a waitlist when permitted by its terms. Placement on a waitlist does not guarantee an award.

## 24. Changes to Award Terms
An award notice should identify material conditions, amount, period, and renewal requirements. Students should retain the notice because a general scholarship policy may not contain the specific terms of an individual award.

## 25. Deferral
A scholarship deferral is available only when the applicable award terms or an authorized decision permit it. Academic leave does not automatically preserve financial aid.

## 26. Withdrawal
If a student withdraws from a programme, the effect on financial aid depends on the award terms and applicable withdrawal procedure. The general policy does not prescribe a universal repayment amount.

## 27. Overpayments
If an administrative error results in an excess scholarship payment, the Financial Aid Office may contact the student and explain the correction process. Students should not treat an unexpected excess payment as a permanent additional award.

## 28. Documentation Deadlines
A scholarship announcement may establish deadlines for income certificates, academic records, identity documents, or other evidence. Missing a deadline may delay review or affect eligibility when the terms provide for that consequence.

## 29. Review of Decisions
A review request should identify the original decision and any new information. A review is not necessarily a new application.

## 30. Conflicting Information
If an application and supporting documents contain inconsistent information, the Financial Aid Office may request clarification. The award decision may be delayed until the discrepancy is resolved.

## 31. Communication
Official scholarship decisions should be communicated through the designated channel. Informal statements by students or unrelated staff should not be treated as final award decisions.

## 32. Closing Principle
Scholarship eligibility is award-specific. The general policy should be used for general procedures, while the individual award notice supplies additional conditions where applicable.
