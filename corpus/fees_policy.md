# Northbridge Institute of Technology
## Student Fees and Payment Policy
**Version:** 2026.1

## 1. General
Students are responsible for paying tuition, hostel, examination, and other applicable fees by the deadlines published by the Institute.

## 2. Semester Fee
For this evaluation corpus, the baseline semester-fee deadline is **15 August**. The fee schedule below is the controlling table within this document.

## 3. Fee Schedule

| Fee Type | Due Date | Standard Late Fee |
|---|---|---:|
| Semester Fee | 15 August | ₹500 |
| Hostel Fee | 20 August | ₹300 |
| Examination Fee | 10 November | ₹200 |
| Library Deposit | 15 August | ₹100 |
| Student Activity Fee | 25 August | ₹100 |

## 4. Late Payment
A late fee may be charged when payment is made after the applicable deadline. Students should retain payment confirmation.

## 5. Payment Failure
If an online payment is debited but not reflected in the student account, the student should submit the transaction reference through the designated support channel. A payment-failure report does not automatically cancel a valid fee deadline.

## 6. Refunds
Refunds are governed by the applicable withdrawal and refund schedule. Some administrative fees may be non-refundable.

## 7. Installments
Installment payment is available only when expressly approved. An installment approval should state the amounts and dates.

## 8. Fee Waivers
Fee waivers may be granted only under a published scheme or authorized decision. Financial difficulty alone does not automatically create a waiver.

## 9. Administrative Holds
Unpaid fees may result in an administrative hold where an Institute notice provides for such a hold. A hold may affect registration or access to selected services.

## 10. Corrections
Students who believe a fee has been incorrectly charged should contact the Finance Office with supporting records. Corrections are considered after verification.

## 11. Notices
The Finance Office may publish revised schedules. Students should check the latest official notice for the applicable academic period.

## 12. Receipts
Students should retain digital or printed payment receipts. A receipt should show the transaction reference and amount where available.

## 13. Third-Party Payments
A payment made by a parent, sponsor, or other third party remains associated with the student's account when correctly identified. Students should verify that the payer uses the correct account information.

## 14. Closing
This document provides baseline fee rules for the evaluation corpus. Where a later official notice expressly changes a deadline, the notice should be considered together with this policy.

## 15. Payment Security
Students should use only payment channels designated by the Institute. They should not share payment credentials or one-time passwords with another person. The Finance Office will not treat a private message as an official payment instruction.

## 16. Duplicate Payments
If a student pays the same fee twice, the student should submit both transaction references. The Finance Office may verify the transactions before processing a correction or refund.

## 17. Failed Transactions
A transaction marked failed by the payment gateway may still require verification if the student's bank account was debited. Students should provide the transaction reference and payment date.

## 18. Fee Certificates
Students may request official fee receipts or certificates through the designated Finance Office process. The request should contain sufficient account information for verification.

## 19. Sponsorship
Where an external sponsor pays a student's fee, the sponsor should follow the payment identification procedure. Sponsorship does not automatically alter the student's liability for a valid fee.

## 20. Registration and Fees
A student should complete fee payment and registration steps specified for the relevant academic period. A payment issue should be reported promptly when it prevents registration.

## 21. Revised Notices
A revised fee notice should identify the academic period, fee category, and effective date. Students should compare the notice with the baseline policy and seek clarification when the two cannot be reconciled.

## 22. Closing Principle
The fee policy describes baseline procedures, but an evaluation system should never silently choose between two incompatible deadlines. It should expose conflicting evidence and state that the corpus does not provide an unambiguous resolution.
